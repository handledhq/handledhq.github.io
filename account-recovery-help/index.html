Questions

Good questions.

How do I get my Instagram account back?

Start at instagram.com/hacked or the “Forgot password?” link on the login screen, using the phone you usually log in on. If the email and phone were changed, ask for the login link to go to the email you first signed up with, and do the video selfie check if Meta offers it. We can walk through it with you.

Can I pay someone to get my Instagram account back?

Yes, but be careful: recovery scams are everywhere. We use only Meta's official steps, give you a free check first, and charge $149 only once you're back in.

How do I contact Instagram for account recovery?

There's no phone number. Recovery goes through the in-app and web recovery flows, the hacked-account page and Meta's support forms. Paid Meta Verified subscribers get a support chat.

How long does Instagram account recovery take?

Some accounts are back in minutes. Identity checks and appeals can take Meta days to answer. We follow up until there's a final answer.

Can you recover a disabled Instagram account?

Sometimes. If Meta lets you appeal, we help you write a clear, honest appeal. If the disable is final, no one can undo it, and we tell you so in the free check.

What if the hacker changed my email and phone number?

Meta usually lets you ask for help using the email you first signed up with, then confirm with a video selfie. It's the most common case we see.

Do you need my password?

No. We work with you on your own device. Once you're back in, you set a new password only you know.

What if you can't get it back?

Then you don't pay. Meta decides, and we say so up front.
