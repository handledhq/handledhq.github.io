What we need from you

What we need to design your flyer.

The words. The headline, the details (date, time, place, prices), and how people reach you. Rough notes are fine; we tidy the wording and you approve it.
Your logo and colors. Whatever you have. A photo of your sign or business card works if that's all there is.
Photos, if you have them. Your own photos of your work, food, team or place look better than stock. If you have none, we use licensed or free-to-use images and tell you which.
Size and where it's going. Letter-size handout, half-page, poster, door hanger, or just social posts. Not sure? Tell us where it'll be seen and we'll pick.
A yes on the quote. Nothing gets started until you've seen the price and said yes.
The honest part

What we don't do.

We don't print or mail it. You get a print-ready PDF that any print shop or office store can print. We can tell you what paper and size to ask for.
We don't copy someone else's art. No logos, characters or photos you don't have the rights to. We use your own material and properly licensed images.
We don't make fake offers or reviews. The prices, deals and quotes on your flyer are yours. We don't add claims you can't back up.
We can't promise how many people it brings in. We make the flyer clear and good-looking. Where it's handed out and what the offer is decide the rest.
