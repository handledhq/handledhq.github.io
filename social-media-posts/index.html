Questions

Good questions.

What do I get for $49?

Seven posts for seven days: each one designed, captioned and scheduled on your account. You see all seven before anything goes up, and you can change any of them.

Where do the posts come from?

From real things: your Google reviews, your own photos, your services and prices, and what's happening in your business this week. We don't invent customers, reviews or results.

Which platforms?

Instagram and Facebook to start. TikTok, Threads, X, LinkedIn or Google Business Profile posts on request.

Do you need my password?

Usually not. The easiest way is to add us as a partner in your free Meta Business Suite, which you can remove anytime. If a platform doesn't allow that, we'll ask for the login the job needs, use it only for posting, and you can change it when we're done. We never ask for bank logins.

Will you buy followers or likes?

No. No bought followers, no fake engagement, no fake reviews. Those get accounts flagged and they don't bring customers.

Do you work with creators?

Yes, for safe-for-work promo posts on Instagram, TikTok and X. We don't handle adult content or log into subscription-site accounts.

Will the posts help me show up on Google?

They can help. Monthly plans include “job in [town]” posts: a real job you just finished, with your customer’s OK, tagged with the town or neighborhood you did it in. They go on your socials and your Google listing, so the towns you serve are backed up with real proof. No one can promise rankings, and we won’t.

What happens after the first week?

Nothing, unless you want more. Keep going at $149 a month for 12 posts, or $249 a month for 20 posts plus stories. Month to month, cancel anytime.
