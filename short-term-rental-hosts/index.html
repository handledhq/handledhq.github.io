Questions

Good questions.

Can you write my Airbnb guidebook?

Yes. Send us what you have, even if it's old texts and sticky notes, and we turn it into a clear guidebook and house manual. It's a one-off project with a fixed quote from Jase before we start.

What is a direct booking website for a vacation rental?

A site of your own with your photos, amenities and reviews, where repeat guests and referrals can check dates and reach you without searching Airbnb. We link it to the booking tool you already use, or add an inquiry form.

Do you take bookings or answer guests for me?

No. We don't manage your calendar, take payments or answer guests live. We write the templates and review replies; you or your existing tools send them.

Will this help my Airbnb ranking?

We can't promise that. Airbnb decides search placement. What we do is make your listing clearer and more accurate, so the guests who read it know what they're getting.

Do you need my Airbnb login?

Only if you want us to paste the changes in for you. Where Airbnb lets you add a co-host with limited permissions, we prefer that. Otherwise we hand you the finished copy to paste in yourself.

I only have one place. Is this for me?

Yes. One place is plenty. Every price is fixed and quoted up front, before we start.
