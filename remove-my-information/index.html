Questions

Good questions.

How do I remove my information from the internet?

Opt out of each people-search site one by one, then use Google's “Results about you” tool to ask Google to remove results showing your address, phone or email. It's free but slow. We do all of it for $99.

Can I hire someone to remove my information from the internet?

Yes. A real person files the opt-outs and Google requests for you and sends you before-and-after screenshots. $99 for the sweep, $29 for a re-check later.

How do I remove my information from the internet for free?

Every opt-out is free if you do it yourself: each people-search site has its own form, and Google's “Results about you” tool is free. It just takes time. We never pay a site to remove you either.

Are data removal services worth it?

Apps charge every year and often can't finish opt-outs that need a phone call or a link from your exact listing. A person can. Either way, sites re-list people, so plan on a re-check.

Can Google remove my personal information?

Google can remove search results that show your home address, phone or email, through its “Results about you” tool. That hides them from Google, but the website still has to be opted out too. We do both.

Can you remove court records or news articles?

No. Government, court and property records and news stories aren't covered by people-search opt-outs.

How long does it take?

Many listings come down within days. Some sites take weeks. Your report says what's done and what's pending.

What do you do with my details?

We use them only to file your opt-outs and delete our working copy when we're done.
