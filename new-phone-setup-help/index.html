Questions

Good questions.

Can someone help me transfer data to my new phone?

Yes. Book a session and we call you at the time you pick. We walk you through moving your contacts, photos, texts and apps, with your OK you share your screen so we see what you see, and we check everything arrived before you wipe the old phone.

How do I transfer data from my old phone to my new phone?

iPhone to iPhone: Apple's Quick Start, which starts when you hold the new iPhone near the old one during setup. Android to Android: the copy apps and data step during setup, using a cable or a QR code; Samsung phones also have Smart Switch. Keep both phones charged, on Wi-Fi and close together until it finishes.

How do I move from iPhone to Android, or Android to iPhone?

iPhone to Android: Google's Switch to Android app. Android to iPhone: Apple's Move to iOS app, chosen on the new iPhone's setup screen. WhatsApp chats need their own transfer step, so do that in the same session.

Does the Apple Store or my carrier help with this?

Apple and your carrier have their own support, in store and online. If you'd rather do it from your own chair at a time you pick, with someone who won't rush you, book a session with us.

Can I transfer data to my new phone after setup?

Often, yes. On an iPhone it usually means resetting the new phone and running setup again, so it's easiest to move everything before you start using it. Samsung's Smart Switch can run after setup. Either way, don't erase the old phone until everything is checked.

Can you transfer data from a phone with a broken screen?

Sometimes. If the phone still turns on, or it was backed up to iCloud or Google, a lot can come back. If it won't turn on at all, we can't repair it or pull data off it, and we'll say so before you pay for more time.

Do you come to my house?
