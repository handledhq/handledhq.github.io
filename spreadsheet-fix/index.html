Can ChatGPT fix my Excel spreadsheet?

It can suggest a formula. It can't see why the totals are off across your tabs, find the one cell typed as text, or check the result against your real numbers. That's the part a person does.

How do I fix my Excel spreadsheet myself?

Start with the error: click the cell with #REF! or #N/A, look at the formula bar, and check that every range still points to the right columns. If totals are off, look for numbers stored as text (they sit on the left of the cell). If it's still wrong, send it to us.

Can you fix a corrupted Excel file?

Often. We try Excel's built-in Open and Repair and the recovery options. If the data can't be recovered, we tell you and refund you in full.
