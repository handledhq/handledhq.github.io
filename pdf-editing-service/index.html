What we do with your PDF.

Free PDF editors are fine for one quick fix. They fall apart on scanned pages, odd fonts, long forms and files that need to look exactly right. That's the job we take.

Change text

Prices, dates, names, phone numbers, typos or a whole paragraph, in the same font and spacing so nobody can tell where the edit was.

Fillable PDF forms

Your paper form turned into a PDF people fill in and sign on screen: text boxes, checkboxes, dropdowns, dates and totals that add up.

Scanned pages made editable

A scan or a photo of a page turned into real text you can search, copy and change. We read it back against the original so nothing gets garbled.

PDF to Word

Your PDF rebuilt as a Word or Google Doc that looks like the original and you can keep editing yourself. Excel too, for tables.

Merge, split, reorder

Several PDFs combined into one, pages pulled out, rotated, put in order and numbered.

Make it smaller

Redesign an old PDF

A tired menu, price list or handout re-laid out so it looks finished. Need it from scratch? Ask about a flyer.

Long documents

Manuals, binders and reports with a table of contents, bookmarks and links that work. Typing up a big stack of paper? See our data entry help.

The honest part

What we don't do.

We don't change official or signed documents. No edits to IDs, certificates, diplomas, transcripts, bank statements, pay stubs, tax forms, medical records, or a contract after it's been signed. Need a corrected one? Ask whoever issued it.
We don't break passwords. If a PDF is locked and it isn't yours, we can't open it. If it is yours and you know the password, we can remove it for you.
We don't sell you software. No PDF editor subscription, no app to install. You get your finished file.
We don't give legal advice. We can turn your form into a fillable one or fix its layout. Whether the wording is right for your situation is a question for a lawyer.

Questions

Good questions.

What are PDF editing services?

Someone else makes the changes to your PDF for you: text and number edits, fillable forms, scanned pages turned into text, PDF to Word, merging, splitting and compressing. With us, a real person does it and checks it against your original.

Can someone edit a PDF for me?

Yes. Send us the PDF and what you want changed. We make the edits in the same fonts and layout and send it back, plus an editable Word copy if you want one.

How much does it cost to hire someone to edit a PDF?

With us, a fixed quote within one business day, before we start, based on how many pages and changes and whether it's a scan. The quote is the price. You don't buy any software or subscription.

Do you have to pay to edit a PDF?

Not always. Free editors handle small changes on simple files. Where they struggle is scanned pages, unusual fonts, long forms and anything that has to look exactly right. That's when it's worth sending it to someone.

Can you turn my PDF into a fillable form?

Yes. We add text boxes, checkboxes, dropdowns, date fields, signature boxes and totals that add up, so people can fill it in on screen and send it back.

Can you convert a PDF to Word?

Yes. We rebuild it as a Word or Google Doc that looks like the original, so you can keep editing it yourself. Scanned PDFs too: we turn the image into real text and check it against the page.

Will you edit any document?

Not quite. We won't change official or signed documents like IDs, certificates, bank statements, pay stubs, transcripts or a contract after it's been signed. Everything else we can do.
