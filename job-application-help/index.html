How much does it cost to pay someone to apply for jobs?

With us, $79 for 25 applications in one week or $279 for 100 over four weeks. Bots are cheaper but send the same resume everywhere; human services usually start around $199.

Is it legal to have someone apply for jobs for you?

Having help filling in applications is common. What matters is that everything is true: your real history, your real contact details, and you doing the interviews and tests yourself. That's how we work.

Is it better than an auto-apply bot?

A bot sends the same resume to as many postings as it can. We read each posting, check it fits, and adjust your resume and answers to it. Fewer, better applications.

Do I get to see the jobs before you apply?

Yes. You approve the list first, or give us a written description of the jobs you want and the ones to skip.

Do you need my passwords?

We need access to the job sites you use, usually your login. We use it only for your applications, never share it, and you can change it when we're done.

Will you do my assessments or interviews?

No. Tests, assessments and interviews are yours. We'll keep the tracker updated so you know which job each one is for.

Can you guarantee I'll get interviews?

No one honest can. We make every application tailored and complete. Employers decide who to call.
