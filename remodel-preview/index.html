Questions

Good questions.

Is the preview what the finished job will look like?

No, it's an idea to get the conversation started. The AI redraws the room in a new style and keeps the layout, but it can invent details, and it doesn't know measurements, codes or what's behind the walls. The real design and price come from the contractor.

What happens to the photo I upload?

It's sent to a free AI service run by Hugging Face to make the preview. We don't save it or see it. Please don't upload photos that show people, house numbers or anything private.

Why did it say I've used up my free previews?

The free AI service gives each internet connection a couple of previews, and they refill over the day. Try again later, or switch from Wi-Fi to cell data.

Which trades is it good for?

Anything you can show in a photo: kitchens, bathrooms, flooring, paint, cabinets, countertops, exteriors, landscaping, pavers and pools.

Can it go on my website?

Yes. We set it up on your site with your colors, your styles and your quote button, and send every request to you. Ask below and we'll show you a version with your name on it.
