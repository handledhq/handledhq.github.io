Questions

Good questions.

How does it work? Do you come to me?

Every session starts with a phone call at the time you pick. Most things we can fix from our computer: with your OK we connect to your device and you watch every step. If we can't fix it from our computer, we'll refund you.

Can you help with my passwords?

Yes. Most fixes need them, so we work through them with you: we reset the ones you've forgotten and set up one simple, safe place to keep them. We use them only while we're with you, and we never keep a copy.

Can my son or daughter book for me?

Yes. Family members can book and pay, and they're welcome to join the call.

Do you fix broken hardware?

No. We don't open up devices or replace parts. If something needs a repair shop, we'll tell you honestly and point you to one.

What should I have ready?

Your phone, tablet or computer charged and nearby, a list of questions if you have one, and any passwords you have written down (don't worry if you've lost them, we can reset them).

What if we don't finish everything?

We'll do the most important things first, send you written notes, and you can book another session whenever you like.
