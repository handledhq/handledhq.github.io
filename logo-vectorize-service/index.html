What it means to vectorize a logo.

A JPG or PNG is made of tiny squares (pixels), so it gets fuzzy when you make it bigger. A vector is made of lines and curves, so it stays crisp at any size. Printers, sign shops and embroiderers ask for one for that reason.

Questions

Good questions.

What does it mean to vectorize a logo?

It means redrawing the logo as lines and curves instead of pixels. A pixel image (JPG or PNG) gets blurry when you make it bigger; a vector stays sharp at any size, from a business card to a banner.

How much does it cost to vectorize a logo?

With us, a fixed quote within one business day, before we start, based on how detailed the logo is. Simple text and shapes take less time than detailed artwork. The quote is the price, and nothing is charged until you say yes.

Can I vectorize a logo for free?

Free auto-trace tools work on simple, sharp logos. On blurry or detailed logos they often make wobbly edges, blobs and wrong letters. If yours comes out like that, or a printer turned it down, send it to us and a person redraws it.

Can you convert my logo to a vector file from a JPG, PNG or photo?

Yes. Send the best copy you have, even a screenshot or a photo of your sign or business card. We redraw it from that.

What files will I get?

SVG and PDF vector files, transparent PNGs in a few sizes, and a white or one-color version for dark backgrounds. If your printer asks for a different format, tell us which and we'll say whether we can send it.

Can I vectorize a logo in Canva?

Canva can download some designs as an SVG, but it doesn't turn a blurry picture into clean vector lines. If your logo started as a JPG or PNG, it needs to be redrawn.

Can you vectorize a logo that isn't mine?

Only if you have the right to use it, such as a team, club or church logo you've been given permission for. We don't redraw other companies' brand logos.

Is there a vectorize logo service near me?

Handled is based in St. George, Utah, and the work is done remotely, so there's nothing to drop off. Send your logo from anywhere in the US.
