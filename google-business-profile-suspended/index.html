Why it happens

Most suspensions come down to a handful of causes.

Google rarely tells you exactly which rule you broke. Finding the real trigger is the most important step, because the appeal has to show it's fixed.

Extra words in your business name

"Smith Plumbing – Best Emergency Plumber St. George" instead of your real, registered name.

An address Google doesn't accept

A P.O. box, a virtual office, a shared suite, or a home address shown on a service-area business.

Recent edits

Changing your name, address, category or hours can trigger a fresh review, and sometimes a suspension.

Duplicate listings

Two profiles for the same business, often one created by an old employee or a past marketing company.

Info that doesn't match

Your website, phone number, signage and registration all tell a slightly different story.

A flag on the Google account

"Suspicious activity" or an account-level issue on the login that manages the profile.

Before you touch anything

Four mistakes that make a suspension worse.

Don't create a new listing.A duplicate while the original is suspended can turn a fixable problem into a permanent one.
Don't appeal before fixing the cause.Your appeal is your best shot. Filing it before the problem is fixed wastes it.
Don't start the appeal without your proof ready.The process can ask you to upload documents on the spot. Have them gathered before you begin.
Don't keep editing the profile.Every change can trigger another review. Make the right changes once.

Questions

Good questions.

Can you guarantee my profile gets reinstated?

No. Google makes the final decision, and anyone who guarantees it is guessing. What we guarantee is that the cause is found and fixed, the evidence is complete, and the appeal is filed correctly.

How long does it take?

It varies. Some appeals are answered within days, others take several weeks. We follow up for you and tell you the moment anything changes.

Will I lose my reviews?

Usually not. When a profile is reinstated, its existing reviews normally come back with it.

Do you need my Google password?

Never. We walk you through adding us as a manager on the profile, which you can remove at any time.

Should I just make a new listing?

No. Creating a duplicate listing while one is suspended can make the situation much worse. Fix and appeal the original.

Same process: we work out what's blocking verification, fix it, and get you through the verification steps. The free review covers it.
