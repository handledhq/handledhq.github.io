What we don't do.

We don't scan prints or come to your house. This is for photos that are already digital. We never take your prints, albums or boxes. Got old prints? Scan them with a free phone scanning app or a local scanning shop first, and we'll organize the files.

Questions

Can I hire someone to organize my photos?

Yes. Tell us where your photos are and how you'd like them sorted. A real person organizes them remotely into albums or folders on your own account. We send a fixed quote within one business day, before we start.

Can I pay someone to organize my photos on my iPhone?

Yes. We sort the Photos app into albums, name the faces, and find duplicates and screenshots for you to check. It all stays in your own iCloud.

Can you organize my Google Photos?

Yes. We make albums by year, person and event, name the faces so search works, fix wrong dates, and set up a shared family album if you want one.

How much does it cost to hire someone to organize photos?

With us, a fixed quote within one business day, before we start, based on how many photos there are and how many places they live. The quote is the price, and nothing is charged until you say yes.

Will you delete any of my photos?

Not without your OK. Duplicates, blurry shots and screenshots go on a list for you to check first. Only the ones you say yes to are removed.

What is the best way to organize photos?

Keep them in one place, sort them into albums by year and event, name the people, clear the duplicates, and add new photos to the right album as you go. That's the setup we build for you, and we show you how to keep it up.

Do you scan old prints?

No. We work with photos that are already digital and never take your prints. Scan them with a free phone scanning app or a local scanning shop, then send us the files to organize.

Is there a photo organizing service near me?

Handled is based in St. George, Utah, and photo organizing is done remotely, so there's nothing to drop off. We can organize your photos from anywhere in the US.
