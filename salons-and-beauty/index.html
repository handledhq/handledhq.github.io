Questions

Good questions.

What do I get for $49?

Seven posts for seven days, each one designed, captioned and scheduled on your account. You see all seven before anything goes up, and you can change any of them.

What should a website for a salon have?

Your services and prices, photos of your own work, your real reviews, your hours and location, and a Book now button that goes to the booking app you already use. On a phone, booking should take one tap.

How much do salon websites cost?

Ours is $288 to build plus $29/mo for hosting and small edits. That's the founding-client price for our first 10 clients (regular $500). We build it first, and you pay only after you've seen it and said yes. Google listing setup is included.

Can you use photos of my clients?

We use the photos you send us. Only send photos your clients are happy to have shared. If you're not sure, we can crop to the hair, nails or lashes, or skip that photo.

Do I have to change my booking app?

No. Keep Vagaro, Square, GlossGenius, Booksy or whatever you use. We put your existing booking link on your posts, your bio, your website and your Google listing.

Do you need my Instagram password?

Usually not. The easiest way is to add us as a partner in your free Meta Business Suite, which you can remove anytime. If that isn't possible, we'll ask for the login the job needs, use it only for posting, and you can change it when we're done. We never ask for bank logins.

I rent a booth or a suite. Is this for me?

Yes. Solo stylists, booth renters and suite owners are exactly who this is for. Your own page, your own work, your own booking link.

Will this get me more bookings?

We can't promise a number, and we won't. What we can do is make sure people who find you see your real work and real reviews, and can book in one tap.
