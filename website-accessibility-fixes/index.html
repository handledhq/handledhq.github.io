Alt text on images

Short, true descriptions so screen-reader users know what each picture shows.

Questions

Good questions.

How do I make my website ADA compliant?

Fix the basics against WCAG 2.1 AA: alt text on images, headings in order, readable color contrast, labeled form boxes, links that say where they go, and everything working with a keyboard. We do that for you for $299 for up to 5 pages. We can't give legal advice or promise you won't be sued.

How much does it cost to make a website ADA compliant?

Agencies usually charge $1,500 to $7,500 for an audit and fixes. Handled charges $299 for up to 5 pages and $39 for each extra page, checked by hand and retested after.

Do I have to make my website ADA compliant?

Many accessibility lawsuits are brought against business websites under the ADA, but whether a rule applies to you is a legal question for a lawyer. What we can do is make your site work for people using screen readers and keyboards.

Do accessibility widgets or overlays work?

Not on their own. They don't fix your actual pages. In 2025 the FTC ordered overlay maker accessiBe to pay $1 million over claims its tool made sites compliant. We never install one.

Can you fix a Squarespace, Wix, GoDaddy or Shopify website?

Yes, using the editing your platform allows. Some parts of some themes are locked; anything we can't change goes in your report so you can ask the platform.

How do I check if my website is accessible?

Free checkers like WAVE or Lighthouse catch some of the problems, not all. The rest needs a person: trying the site with only a keyboard and a screen reader. We do both and send you the results.

Is there such a thing as ADA certification for a website?

No. There's no official ADA certificate for websites. Anyone selling one is selling a badge. We tell you what we fixed and what we couldn't.
