Questions

Good questions.

Can I hire someone to sell my stuff on Facebook Marketplace?

Yes. Send us phone photos of each item and what you know about it. We clean up the photos, write the listing and post it on your own Facebook account. Buyers message you and pay you. You handle the pickup or shipping.

How much does a Facebook Marketplace listing service cost?

With us, a fixed quote within one business day, before we start, based on how many items you have. The quote is the price. We don't take a cut of what sells.

Do you take a cut of the sale?

No. You pay the fixed quote for the listing work. Buyers pay you directly, so we never touch your sale money or your payouts.

How do I get paid if I sell something on Facebook Marketplace?

For local pickup, the buyer pays you in person, usually cash or a payment app. For items you ship, buyers pay through Facebook checkout and Facebook pays out to the account you set up. Either way the money goes to you, not us.

How do I renew my listing on Facebook Marketplace?

Facebook shows a renew option on older listings in your Marketplace selling page, under Your listings. If you'd rather not keep track, we can quote renewing and repricing what hasn't sold.

Do you need my Facebook password?

We need access to post on your account, which usually means the login. We use it only for your listings, never share it, and you can change it when we're done. We never ask for bank logins.

Can you sell my things on your account instead?

No. Every listing goes on your own account, in your name. That keeps the buyer messages, the money and the reviews with you.

Is there a Facebook Marketplace listing service near me?
