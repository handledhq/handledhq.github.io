Questions

Good questions.

Who do you text?

Only your own past customers: people who hired you or asked you for a quote and gave you their number. We never text bought lists, strangers or anyone who has asked you to stop.

Is this a bot that chats with my customers?

No. We write the texts with you, you approve every word, and they go out from your business with your name on them. When someone replies, it comes straight to you. Nothing pretends to be a person.

What does it cost?

$299 for the campaign: list cleanup, the offer and messages, setup and sending, and the results report. Carrier texting fees are passed through at cost. We tell you the exact number before anything sends; for a list of about 500 people it's usually under $50, including the one-time carrier registration.

Do you guarantee bookings?

No. Nobody honest can promise how many people reply. What we promise: a clean list, a good offer, texts that follow the rules, every reply handed to you fast, and a plain report of what happened.

Why does it take a week or two the first time?

US phone carriers require businesses to register before sending texts from a business number. We do the paperwork in your business name. If the tool you already use (like Jobber or Housecall Pro) can already text your customers, we send from there and skip the wait.

What if someone says stop?

They're removed right away and never texted again. Every first message tells people how to opt out, and we only send at reasonable hours.

Can you ask for Google reviews too?

Yes, but the honest way: we ask every customer the same way, not just the happy ones. Google doesn't allow picking who gets asked, and it doesn't allow rewards for reviews, so we don't do either.

Can we do it by email instead?

Yes. If texting isn't a fit, we can run the same win-back by email for the same price, with no carrier fees and no registration wait. Texts usually get read more, so we'll tell you which we'd pick for your list.
