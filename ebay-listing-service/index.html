Questions

Good questions.

Can I pay someone to list my items on eBay?

Yes. Send us phone photos of each item and what you know about it. We clean up the photos, write the title, description and item specifics, and post the listing on your own eBay account. Buyers pay you through eBay. You ship.

How much do you pay someone to list on eBay?

With us, a fixed quote within one business day, before we start, based on how many items you have. The quote is the price. eBay charges its usual selling fees the same as if you listed it yourself, and the sale money goes to you.

Do you take a cut of what sells?

No. You pay the fixed quote for the listing work. eBay pays you directly, so we never touch your sale money or your payouts.

Is there an eBay listing service near me?

Handled is based in St. George, Utah, and the work is done remotely, so there is nothing to drop off. You keep your items and send us photos from anywhere in the US.

Can someone sell on eBay for me if I don't have an account?

We can help you set up your own eBay account first, in your name, with your own payout details entered by you. Then we list on it. We never sell your things on our account.

Do you need my eBay password?

We need access to post on your account, which usually means the login. We use it only for your listings, never share it, and you can change it when we're done. We never ask for bank logins.

Auction or Buy It Now?

You choose. We look up what the same item has actually sold for on eBay and suggest a price and a format. Most everyday items do well as Buy It Now with Best Offer turned on.

Do you pick up, store or ship my items?

No. Your items stay with you the whole time. When something sells, eBay gives you the shipping label and you pack it and drop it off.
