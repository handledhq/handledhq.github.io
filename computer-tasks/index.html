Questions

Good questions.

Can I hire someone to do computer work for just one job?

Yes. That's what this is. You don't sign up for a plan or buy a block of hours. Tell us the one job, we send a fixed quote, and if you say yes we do it.

How much does it cost to hire someone to do a small computer task?

Every one-off job gets a fixed quote within one business day, before we start. The quote is the price. If a job grows, we ask before doing more, and you can say no.

Can I pay someone to sell my stuff online?

Yes. Send us photos of the items and what you know about them. We write the titles and descriptions and post them on your own Poshmark, Mercari, Depop, eBay or Facebook Marketplace account. Buyers pay you through the platform. You ship. We never hold your money or your items.

Do you use AI photos for listings?

Real photos of the actual item always come first, flaws included. If you want them, AI on-model or styled shots go after those as extras, matched to the real item's color, pattern and size, and never used to hide wear. Honest photos mean fewer returns.

Do you need my passwords?

For some jobs, yes: we ask for the logins that job needs, or for user access where the tool offers it. We use them only for your job, never share them, and you can change them when we're done. We never ask for bank logins.

Do you fix computers or build PCs?

No. We don't repair hardware, build PCs or come to your house. We do the work that happens on a screen. For help with your own phone or computer, see our tech help page.

Are you local, or is this done remotely?

Handled is based in St. George, Utah. The work itself is done remotely, so you can send us a job from anywhere in the US.

What if I don't know exactly what I need?

Tell us the end result you want, in your own words. A voice memo or a photo of a messy sheet is fine. We'll come back with what the job is and the fixed price.
