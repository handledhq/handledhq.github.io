Questions

Can I pay someone to do my family tree?

Yes. Tell us what you know, and we research up to 4 generations in free records like FamilySearch, with a source for every name. You get a PDF chart and your FamilySearch tree filled in if you want. $149.

How much does it cost to have someone do your genealogy?

Research firms usually charge about $300 to $800 for a basic package, and independent genealogists about $50 to $200 an hour. Our starter tree is $149 for up to 4 generations.

Can you hire a genealogist through Ancestry?

Ancestry has its own paid research service, AncestryProGenealogists, for bigger projects. Our starter tree uses free records, so you don't need an Ancestry subscription.

Are you professional genealogists?

No. We're careful researchers using free records, and we say so up front. For legal or lineage-society proof, hire an accredited genealogist.

Is FamilySearch really free?

Yes. FamilySearch is a free family history site run by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. Anyone can make a free account.

How far back can you go?

The starter tree covers up to 4 generations, back to your great-grandparents, as far as the records allow. Going further back is quoted first.

Do you do DNA genealogy?

No. Interpreting DNA matches is a specialist job, and we don't take it on.
