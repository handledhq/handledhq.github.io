Can I pay someone to do my genealogy?

Can I pay someone to do my family tree?

Yes. Our starter tree goes from you back to your great-grandparents, as far as the free records allow, and comes as a PDF chart plus your FamilySearch tree if you want it.

How much does it cost to have someone do your genealogy?

Professional genealogists usually charge about $50 to $200 an hour, and basic family tree packages often run $300 to $800. Our starter family tree is $149.

Are you professional genealogists?

No. We're not accredited genealogists, and we say so up front. We're careful researchers who cite the record behind every name, so anyone can check our work. For legal or lineage-society proof, hire an accredited genealogist.

What records do you use?

Free online records, mainly FamilySearch: census, birth, marriage, death and burial records. We don't pay for records without your OK.

What if you hit a brick wall?

We tell you where the records stop and write a short note on where to look next, instead of guessing.

Can you help with my DNA results?

No. We don't interpret DNA tests or ethnicity reports. Our tree is built from records.

Do I need an Ancestry or FamilySearch account?

No paid account is needed. If you want the tree on FamilySearch, a free account is enough, and we can help you set one up.
