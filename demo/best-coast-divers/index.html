Who's under your boat

Family owned. In the water since 2015.

Best Coast Divers is a family-owned hull cleaning company run by Chad Miller, owner and dive supervisor, and his wife. We've been diving San Diego marinas since 2015.

The Port of San Diego requires every hull cleaning business working in the bay to hold a permit. Ours is permit #62828, and Best Coast Divers is on the Port's current list of permitted hull cleaners. When you call or text, you're talking to the people who do the work.

Questions

Before you call

Does my boat have to come out of the water?

No. Hull cleaning, zincs, prop service and video inspections are all done by divers in your slip.

Are you permitted to clean hulls in San Diego Bay?

Yes. The Port of San Diego requires every hull cleaning business in the bay to hold a permit. Best Coast Divers holds permit #62828 and is on the Port's list of permitted hull cleaning businesses.

Where do you work?

All over San Diego. Text your marina and we'll tell you if we can get there.

How long have you been doing this?

Since 2015. Best Coast Divers is family owned and run by Chad Miller, owner and dive supervisor.

Are you hiring?

Yes, divers. No experience needed, and training is provided. See careers.

How do I get a price?

Text or call 619-916-6767 with your boat's length and type, your marina and slip, and what you need. The form above writes that text for you.
