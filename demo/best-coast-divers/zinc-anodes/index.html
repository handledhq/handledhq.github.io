Why it matters

Below the waterline

A zinc anode is sacrificial: it corrodes so your underwater metal doesn't. That's the job.

Once a zinc is used up it stops protecting anything, and galvanic corrosion moves on to the parts that cost real money: props, shafts and fittings.

Salt water eats zincs constantly, so they need regular checks. Ask Chad how often he'd check yours.
