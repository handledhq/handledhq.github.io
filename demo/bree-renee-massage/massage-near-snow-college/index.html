Questions

Before you book

How far is Bree's studio from Snow College?

41 W 700 S, Lower Level, Ephraim is a few minutes from campus. Snow College students and staff are right here in Ephraim. The studio is on the south end of town.

Do you do in-home or mobile massage?

No. Every session is in Bree's quiet studio in Ephraim, so she can give you the full experience: warm linens, aromatherapy and a calm room.

When are you open?

Sundays and Wednesdays 11 am to 8:30 pm, and Saturdays 11 am to 9 pm, by appointment.

How much is a massage?

Swedish massage starts at $111 and deep tissue at $122 for 60 minutes. Reiki is $65 a session.
