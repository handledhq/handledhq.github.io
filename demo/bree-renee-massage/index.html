Meet Bree

Massage is my art, my passion & my purpose

Bree followed a string of "nudges" to massage school, having received only one massage in her life. The minute she walked through the doors of Healing Mountain Massage School, she knew she was home.

Today she's a licensed massage therapist and Reiki Master who blends skilled bodywork with energetic awareness. Her purpose is simple: help every client feel cared for, safe and loved.

Questions

Good to know

Where are you located?

In Ephraim, Utah (718 N 80 E). Clients come from all over Sanpete County: Manti, Mount Pleasant, Gunnison, Fairview and Snow College.

How do I book?

Book online any time with the Book button. You'll pick your massage, length and time, and get a confirmation right away. You can also text Bree.

Which massage should I choose?

Swedish if you want to relax and reset. Deep tissue if you have knots, pain or tension that keeps coming back. Not sure? Book either one and Bree will tailor it to you.

Do you take new male clients?

New male clients are welcomed by referral from an existing client.

Can I talk, or stay quiet?

Either. Talk or stay silent your whole massage. It's okay if you cry. Bree is there to support you.
