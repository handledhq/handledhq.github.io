Questions

Before you book

Does deep tissue massage hurt?

It should feel intense in a good way, never sharp. Bree checks in with you and adjusts the pressure the whole time.

What is trigger point therapy?

Steady pressure on a tight knot until it softens. It's part of every deep tissue session with Bree.

Will I be sore afterward?

Some people feel a little tender the next day, a bit like after a good workout. Drink water and keep moving gently.

Can I add hot stones?

Yes. Hot stones let Bree work deeper into the muscles without intense pressure. Add them when you book.
