Questions

Before you book

How much pressure is Swedish massage?

Light to medium. Bree checks in during your session, so you can always ask for more or less pressure and she won't be offended.

How long should my first session be?

Most people love 90 minutes, because it leaves time for the whole body without rushing. 60 minutes works well for regular upkeep, and 120 minutes is a deep rest.

What should I wear?

Undress to your comfort level. You stay covered with a sheet the whole time and only the area being worked on is uncovered.

Can I just relax and not talk?

Yes. Talk or stay silent your whole massage. Bree is there for you.
