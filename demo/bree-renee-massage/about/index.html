My story

I found my purpose in healing massage

If I do nothing else in this life but help people feel cared for and loved, I've fulfilled my purpose.

I received several "nudges" from the Universe to drop out of college and pursue massage school, having only received one massage in my entire life. Still, I couldn't shake the feeling that this was my path.

The very minute I walked through the doors of Healing Mountain Massage School I knew I was in the right place. When we started the hands-on portion of the curriculum, I knew I had found my home.

Throughout my life, touch had always felt unsafe or had expectations attached. To experience touch in safety, love and care healed me deep down to my bones. I knew I had found my purpose: to create the same impact for others.

Now, with every client who walks into my space, I hope to give them that same experience. From the way I handle sheets, to the aromatherapy I've chosen, to setting intentions, all your "mess" is left at the door so you can simply be.

A few promises

  • I don't care what your body looks like. I've worked with all shapes and sizes and I honor your body where it's at.
  • Tell me if you want more or less pressure. I promise I won't be offended.
  • Talk or stay silent your whole massage. I'm here for you.
  • It's okay if you cry. I'm here to support you.
