Before your appointment

How to prepare

01

Shave the day before

The laser needs the root, not the hair on top.

02

No waxing or plucking

For about 4 weeks before. It removes the root we target.

03

Skip the tan

No sunbathing or self-tanner for 2 weeks before treatment.

04

Come clean

No deodorant, lotion or oils on the area that day.

FAQ

Good questions

How many sessions will I need?

Most people need a series of treatments spaced several weeks apart. Jessica will build a plan for your hair and skin type.

Does it hurt?

Most clients describe a quick snap with cooling right after. It's far quicker than waxing.

When should I start?

Fall and winter are ideal: less sun on treated skin, and you'll finish your series before swimsuit season.
