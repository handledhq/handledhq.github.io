FAQ

Good questions

Does Morpheus8 hurt?

Numbing cream is applied first. Most clients describe a warm, prickly feeling. Jessica will talk you through every step.

Is there downtime?

Expect redness for a day or two after Morpheus8, like a mild sunburn. Forma has no downtime.

How many treatments will I need?

It depends on your skin and goals. Many people see a change after one Morpheus8 treatment, and a series gives the best results. You'll get a plan at your consultation.

What's the aftercare?

No makeup for 24 hours, gentle cleanser and the post-care Jessica sends home with you, no workouts, hot tubs or saunas for 48 hours, and SPF every day.
