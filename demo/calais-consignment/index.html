Questions

The easy answers.

Do I have to fill anything out?

No. No forms and no questionnaire. Send photos of what you want to sell and Calais will take it from there.

Do my photos need to be good?

Not at all. Phone pictures on the bed or the floor are perfect. Calais takes the real listing photos herself.

Where does Calais work?

Photos can come from anywhere. For house calls, she's mostly in the Las Vegas and San Diego areas. Ask about anywhere else.

Do I need the box or receipt?

No, but if you have them, include them. Boxes, dust bags and receipts help a piece sell for more.

What if I'm not sure something is worth selling?

Send it anyway. That's what Calais is for.
