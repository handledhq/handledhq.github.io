Watch the sprinklers

Sprinklers that hit the glass leave mineral spots that bake on in the sun. Aim the heads away from windows.

Screens and tracks count

Dirty screens and tracks put dust right back on clean glass.

Stay on a schedule

Regular cleanings keep buildup from turning into spots that are hard to remove.
