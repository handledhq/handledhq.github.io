Questions

Good questions.

What is local SEO?

It's everything that decides whether you show up when someone nearby searches for your service: mainly your Google Business Profile, your reviews, and your website's service and town pages.

How long does local SEO take?

Profile fixes can show up within weeks. Ranking in the top three on the map usually takes one to three months of steady work, and depends on your competition.

Do you ask for my Google password?

We'll usually ask you to add us as a manager on the profile, because it's the cleanest way and you can remove us anytime. If that's not possible, we'll use your Google login for the fix and you can change the password when we're done.

How much does local SEO cost?

Google listing setup and optimization is $149 flat, after a free review. Fixing your name, phone and address across 10 major directories is $149 flat. Both are included if you have a website with us, and the $149 is credited back if you start one within 30 days.

Do you only ask happy customers for reviews?

No. We ask every customer. Filtering out unhappy customers is against Google's rules and can get a listing penalized.
