About the photos

Real photos first. Always.

The real item leads. The first photos on every listing are of the actual item, front, back, tag and any flaw. Better light and a clean background, yes. Editing out a stain, a pull or a fade, never.
AI shots are extras. If you want on-model or styled photos, they go after the real ones. They have to match the item exactly: same color, same pattern, same length. They're labeled as styled images where the app asks for that.
Why it matters. A buyer who gets what they saw in the photos has no reason to send it back. Honest listings mean fewer returns and better reviews on your closet.

Questions

Good questions.

Can I hire someone to sell my clothes online?

Yes. Send us phone photos of the clothes and what you know about them (brand, size, how often they were worn). We edit the photos, write the titles and descriptions, and post them on your own Poshmark, Mercari, Depop, eBay or Facebook Marketplace account. Buyers pay you through the app. You ship.

How much should I pay someone to sell my stuff?

With us, a fixed quote within one business day, before we start, based on how many items and which apps. The quote is the price. The money from each sale goes to you through the platform, and the platform takes its usual selling fee the same as if you'd posted it yourself.

Do you take a cut of my sales?

No. You pay the fixed quote for the listing work. Buyers pay you through the app, so we never touch your sale money or your payouts.

Is this a Poshmark listing service or a crosslisting service?

Both. We can list on just one app, or post the same item on several (Poshmark, Mercari, Depop, eBay, Facebook Marketplace) so more buyers see it. If an item sells on one app, you mark it sold or delete it on the others, or we can do that while we're helping.

Do you use AI product photos?

Only as extras, and only if you want them. The first photos are always real photos of the actual item, flaws included. AI on-model or styled shots go after those, matched to the item's real color, pattern and length, never used to hide wear, and labeled as styled where the app asks.

Do you need my Poshmark or eBay password?

We need access to post on your account: the login, or user access where the app offers it. We use it only for your listings, never share it, and you can change it when we're done. We never ask for bank logins.

Is there someone near me who can sell my clothes online?

Handled is based in St. George, Utah, and the work is done remotely, so you don't need to drop anything off. You keep your items and send us photos from anywhere in the US.

Do you pick up, store or ship my items?

No. Your items stay with you the whole time. When something sells, the app gives you the shipping label and you pack and send it.
