Questions

Good questions.

How do I edit my website on GoDaddy?

Log in to GoDaddy, open My Products, find Websites + Marketing (or your WordPress site) and choose Edit Website. Click the section you want to change, edit it, then Publish. If you'd rather not, send us the list.

How do I edit my Squarespace website?

Log in, open the site, choose Pages, click Edit on the page, change the block, then Save. Hours and contact details are often in the footer, which you edit separately.

How do I edit my website in WordPress?

Log in at your site's /wp-admin, open Pages, edit the page and click Update. Some themes keep phone numbers and hours in the Customizer or a theme options page.

How much do website updates cost?

Do I need to give you my password?

Not if your platform lets you add a contributor or user, which Squarespace, Wix and WordPress do. Otherwise your login works, and you can change it when we're done.

The person who built my website disappeared. Can you still help?

Yes, if the site is in your name. If the old web person still controls the account or domain, we help you ask them or the platform for access in your name.
