Questions

Good questions.

What does it cost?

$288 one-time to set it all up, then $147 a month for the first 6 months, then $97 a month after that. Cancel any month after the first 3. Your texting app and carrier fees are paid by you directly, at cost, and we tell you the exact number first.

When do I pay?

After you've seen your new website and said yes. We build it first. If you don't like it, you don't pay.

Do you guarantee more jobs?

No. Nobody honest can promise how many people call. What we promise: every piece set up properly, working every month, and a plain one-page report so you can see what it's doing.

Is a robot answering my customers?

No. Texts go out from your business with your name on them, and when someone replies, it comes straight to you. No robot voice, no chatbot pretending to be your office.

Can I buy just one piece?

Yes. Every piece is also sold on its own. If only one thing is broken, fix that one thing. The Growth System is for owners with two or more leaks who want it all done together for one monthly price.

Do I need to run ads?

No. The whole point is to get more out of the customers, calls and searches you already have before you spend a dollar on ads.

How do you ask for reviews?

Every finished job gets the same polite text with your Google review link. We never ask only the happy customers and never offer anything in return for a review. Google doesn't allow either. If someone replies unhappy, it goes to you so you can make it right.

Why does texting take a week or two to start?

US phone carriers require businesses to register before texting from a business number. We do the paperwork in your business name once, and all the texting pieces use that one number. If your job app can already text customers, we use it and skip the wait.
