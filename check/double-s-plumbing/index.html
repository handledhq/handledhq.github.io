What's tripping it up

In plain words, with how to fix each one yourself. This is yours to keep.

Pictures have no description 4 spots

Why it matters: Screen readers used by blind customers just say “image”, so they miss your work photos.

Fix it yourself: In your site editor, open each image's settings and fill in the alt text with a few words saying what the photo shows, like “new water heater installed in a St. George garage”.

The page doesn't say what language it's in 1 spot

Why it matters: Screen readers may read it with the wrong accent or pronunciation.

Fix it yourself: Set the site language to English in your platform settings (or add lang="en" to the html tag).

Honest note: an automated test only catches part of the problems, and a score isn't a legal opinion. A person checking with a keyboard and a screen reader finds the rest.
