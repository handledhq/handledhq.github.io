Hiring pool service company help in Washington

Washington sits just east of St. George, about 10 minutes up I-15 or along Telegraph Street, so many St. George pros cover it too. Neighborhoods these pool service companies work in include Green Springs, Coral Canyon, Sienna Hills, Washington Fields and the neighborhoods along Telegraph Street. You'll know you're in the right area near Washington Dam Road, the Green Springs golf course and I-15 exits 10, 13 and 16.

A lot of Washington's homes are newer subdivisions in Coral Canyon and Washington Fields, so warranty work and first repairs come up often.

Washington was settled in 1857 by cotton missionaries, and the old Washington Cotton Factory still stands on Telegraph Street. Much of the old farmland in Washington Fields is now homes, and the city is one of the fastest-growing in the county.
