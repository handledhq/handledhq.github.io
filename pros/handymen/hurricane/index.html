Hiring handyman help in Hurricane

Hurricane is about 25 minutes from St. George, off I-15 exit 16 and up State Route 9, so a pro based in town can usually get to you sooner. Neighborhoods these handymen work in include the streets off State Street and Main Street, the Sky Mountain area and the newer builds toward Sand Hollow. You'll know you're in the right area near Sand Hollow State Park, Quail Creek State Park, Sky Mountain Golf Course and State Route 9 toward Zion.

With homes spread from Sky Mountain out toward Sand Hollow, ask a pro which side of town they cover before you book.

Hurricane is named for the whirlwind that blew the top off Erastus Snow's buggy on Hurricane Hill in the 1860s, and the Hurricane Canal, dug by hand by early settlers, is what made farming here possible. Today it's a common home base for Zion visitors, so many houses here are vacation rentals that need quick work between guests.
