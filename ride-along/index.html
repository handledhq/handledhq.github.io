Questions

Good questions.

Is it really free?

Yes. The call and the hand-off list are free, and there's no obligation to buy anything.

Do I have to give you any passwords?

No. You share your screen and stay in control. We never log in to anything during a ride-along, and we never ask for bank logins, ever.

What if I don't want to show certain things?

Then don't. Close anything private before you share, and skip whatever you like. We only need to see the kind of work, not your numbers.

Who is this for?

Contractors and home-service businesses first, but any small business owner whose evenings disappear into computer work is welcome.

What happens after?

You get the list. If something on it is worth handing off, reply and we start. If not, keep the list. It's yours.
