Emails going to spam?

Spam isn't one problem.

Missing SPF. Nothing tells other inboxes which services are allowed to send mail for your domain. We add it.
Missing DKIM. Your mail isn't signed, so receiving inboxes can't confirm it wasn't changed on the way. We turn it on.
No DMARC. Gmail and Yahoo now check for it, and mail without it is more likely to be filtered. We set up DMARC and start it gently so nothing real gets blocked.
Too many senders. Your website form, invoicing app and newsletter tool all send as you. We make sure each one is allowed.

Questions

Good questions.

Google Workspace or Microsoft 365: which one?

If you already live in Gmail, Google Workspace. If you already use Word, Excel and Outlook, Microsoft 365. Both work well for a small business. We'll tell you which fits and why before you pay.

Why are my emails going to spam?

Most of the time your domain is missing one of three settings: SPF, DKIM or DMARC. Without them, other inboxes can't confirm the mail is really from you. Sometimes it's a website form or app sending as you without permission. We find which one it is and fix it.

What is DMARC, in plain English?

A short setting on your domain that tells Gmail, Outlook and others what to do with mail that claims to be from you but isn't. Gmail and Yahoo now check for it. We set up DMARC and start it gently, so your real mail keeps flowing while fake mail gets caught.

What does the mailbox subscription cost?

That's between you and Google or Microsoft, billed straight to you at their published price per person, per month. We don't resell it or add anything on top. Our $149 is one time.

Will I lose my old emails?

No. We can move your old mail and contacts into the new account. If your old email is still running, it keeps working until the switch, so nothing gets missed.

What do you need from me?

Access to wherever your domain is managed (GoDaddy, Squarespace, Namecheap and so on) and to your current email, either by adding us as a user or by giving us the logins the job needs, which you can change when we're done. We never ask for bank logins.

How long does it take?

Our part usually takes a day or two. New domain settings can take a few hours, sometimes up to a couple of days, to work everywhere. We keep checking and tell you when it's all through.

Can you guarantee my emails won't go to spam?

No one honestly can. Each inbox provider makes its own call. We fix everything on your side, test it with real inboxes, and show you the results.
