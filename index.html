Questions

Good questions.

How can you do it so fast?

Because this is all we do. We've built our own tools and processes for exactly this kind of work, so jobs that take most people weeks take us days.

How much is a website?

$500 one-time for the build, plus $29 a month for hosting and small edits. We build it first and you see it finished before you pay anything. Rather pay nothing upfront? Choose $0 down and $149 a month, all-in. No long contract either way, and if you leave we hand you your content.

Do I have to be local?

No. We work with businesses and people nationwide. Everything happens online, by email, Instagram message, or a quick video call if you want one.

What if I'm not happy with it?

We revise until it's right. It isn't done until you say it's handled.

How will I know it's working?

Every month you get a short, plain-English report: calls, form fills, reviews and where you show up on Google. Not a page of jargon and charts.

What can't you do?

We don't give legal, tax or financial advice, and we won't do anything that requires a licence we don't hold. If it's outside our lane, we'll tell you up front.

What's a ride-along?

A free 30-minute video call where you share your screen and do your normal office work while we watch. Afterwards you get a written list of what we'd take off your plate, each with a fixed price. You never give us a password, and the list is yours to keep either way. Book one here.

Do you have a referral deal?

Yes. When a business you refer signs up for a monthly plan, your next month is free. They just need to mention your name.

Is my information kept private?

Yes. Your files are used only for your job and never shared or shown to anyone without your written OK.
