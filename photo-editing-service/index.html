Questions

Can I pay someone to edit my photos?

Yes. Send us the photos and what you want changed. We edit them by hand and send them back full size, for $9 a photo.

How much do photo editors charge?

Human retouching services usually charge about $5 to $30 a photo, and old photo restoration often runs $35 to $85. With us, $9 a photo for everyday edits and $29 to restore an old one.

Can you edit my wedding photos?

Yes, for color, light, cropping and small touch-ups on your own photos. If your photographer's contract limits edits, check it first.

Can you remove the background from product photos?

Yes. White or transparent backgrounds, sized for Etsy, Amazon, Shopify or eBay. The product itself stays exactly as it is.

Can you fix old photos?

Yes, for $29 a photo from a good scan: scratches, dust, fading and small tears. Badly damaged parts can be improved, not rebuilt from nothing.

Do you edit real estate photos?

Yes: brightness, straightening and color. We don't hide damage or change what the property looks like.

How do I send you my photos?

Share a Google Drive, Dropbox or iCloud link to the full-size files, or email them. Texted photos lose quality.

Do you keep my photos?

No. We use them only for your job and delete our copies when you've got the finished files.
