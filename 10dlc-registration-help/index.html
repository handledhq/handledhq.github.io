Campaign rejected?

“Rejected” usually means one of these.

The business details don't match. The legal name or EIN on the form isn't exactly what the IRS has. We match it and resubmit.
The opt-in can't be checked. Reviewers couldn't see how customers agreed to get texts. We put the opt-in wording on your site where they can see it.
The privacy policy is missing a line. Carriers look for a statement that phone numbers aren't shared or sold for marketing. We add an SMS section that says so.
The samples or website don't line up. Sample texts without your name or opt-out, or a website that's down or doesn't match the business. We fix the samples and flag what your site needs.

Questions

Good questions.

What is A2P 10DLC, in plain English?

It's the registration US phone carriers require before a business can text customers from a regular 10-digit business number. You register your business (the brand) and what you'll be texting (the campaign). Without it, your texts get blocked or filtered.

My A2P 10DLC campaign was rejected. Can you fix it?

Usually. Most rejections come down to a few things: business details that don't match IRS records, an opt-in reviewers can't see, a privacy policy missing the SMS section, or sample messages missing your name or opt-out. We find which one it is, fix it and resubmit. If it's still rejected after our fix, you don't pay the $99.

Do you write the SMS privacy policy for 10DLC?

Yes. We add an SMS section to your privacy policy and a texting terms page, using the published templates and guidelines carriers and the industry provide, and put the opt-in wording on your website. It's not legal advice; if your business has special rules, have a lawyer look it over.

Which texting platforms do you work with?

Most of the common ones small businesses use, including platforms like Twilio and the texting built into many business apps. Tell us what you use in the form and we'll confirm before you pay.

What does registration cost besides your fee?

Your texting platform charges small registration fees: usually a one-time brand fee, a one-time campaign review fee and a monthly campaign fee. They're billed by the platform straight to you. We tell you the exact amounts before we submit.

How long does approval take?

Our part takes a day or two. The carriers' review often takes a few business days and sometimes a couple of weeks. They make the final call, so we can't promise a date, but you'll always know where it stands.

What do you need from me?

Your legal business name, EIN and address as the IRS has them, your website, and access to your texting platform and website, either as a user or with the logins the job needs, which you can change when we're done. We never ask for bank logins.

I don't have a website. Can I still register?

Carriers check that a real business is behind the texts, and a website makes approval much easier. If you don't have one, we'll tell you your options, including a website from us.
