The honest part

What we don't do.

We don't hold your money. Buyers pay through your store. We never touch your sales, your payouts or your bank.

We don't hide flaws or fake photos. Real photos of the actual product come first. Any AI-styled shot is an extra that must match the item exactly, and we never use one to hide wear or change what it looks like.
We don't promise sales or rankings. We make each listing as good as it can be. Where it shows up and who buys is up to the site and its buyers.

Questions

Good questions.

What are product listing services?

Someone else writes and uploads your product listings for you: titles, descriptions, tags, photos, prices and options, on Etsy, Amazon, Shopify, eBay or wherever you sell. With us, a real person does it on your own seller account from the photos and details you send.

How much do product listing services cost?

With us, a fixed quote within one business day, before we start, based on how many products, which sites and how much detail you already have. The quote is the price, and we don't take a cut of your sales. A brand-new Shopify store with up to 25 products is $299.

Can I pay someone to list my products on Etsy?

Yes. Send us photos and details for each product. We write the title, all 13 tags, attributes and description, and upload the listing to your own Etsy shop. Orders and payouts go to you.

Can you list my products on Amazon?

Yes, in your own Amazon Seller Central account. We write the title, bullet points, description and search terms from your details and follow Amazon's rules for your category. You need your own seller account, and we can't get you approved for restricted categories.

Do you need my password?

Etsy, Shopify and Amazon all let you add someone as staff or a user, which is the safest way. If a site doesn't, we use the login only for your listings, never share it, and you can change it when we're done. We never ask for bank logins.

Can you take products from one site and list them on another?

Yes. If your products are already on Etsy or Shopify, we can copy and adapt the listings for Amazon, eBay or another site, so each one fits how that site's buyers search.

Do you use AI photos?

Real photos of the actual product always come first. If you want an AI-styled lifestyle or on-model shot as an extra, it has to match the product exactly and never hide wear. That means fewer returns and better reviews.

Are there product listing services near me?

Handled is based in St. George, Utah, and the work is done remotely, so there is nothing to drop off. Send us photos and details from anywhere in the US.
