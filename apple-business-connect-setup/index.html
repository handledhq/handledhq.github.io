Questions

Good questions.

Why does Apple Maps matter?

Every iPhone opens addresses in Apple Maps, and Siri pulls business details from it. If your place card is missing, wrong or unclaimed, iPhone users see whatever Apple guessed: old hours, an old number, or a competitor nearby.

Isn't this the same as my Google listing?

No. Apple keeps its own business listings in Apple Business Connect. Fixing Google doesn't fix Apple. Most local businesses have never claimed theirs.

My verification has said "pending" for weeks. Can you fix it?

Usually. "Pending" can mean a few different things: Apple is checking your business itself, it's trying to confirm you control a place that's already on Maps, or someone else already manages that place. We find out which one it is, fix what's blocking it (often the phone number Apple has on file, or a missed verification call), and take the next step with Apple. Apple makes the final call, so we can't promise a date, but you'll know exactly where it stands.

How long does it take?

Our part takes a day or two. Apple's review usually takes a few business days, and sometimes one to two weeks. We keep checking and tell you when it's through.

What do you need from me?

Your business details, a few good photos, and access: either you add us as a user in your Apple Business Connect account, or you give us the login the job needs, which you can change when we're done. Sometimes Apple calls the business number to verify, so we'll tell you when to expect the call. We never ask for bank logins.

What if you can't fix the stuck verification?

If we can't find anything to fix and move it forward, you don't pay the $49.
